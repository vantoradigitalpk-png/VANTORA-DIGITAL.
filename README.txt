VANTORA DIGITAL WEBSITE PACKAGE
================================

Upload the contents of this folder to your hosting web root (often public_html). Keep index.html at the root and upload the assets folder too.

Includes 13 pages: Home, About, Services, six individual service pages, Portfolio, Pricing, Reviews, Blog and Contact.

Features: responsive dark/red design, animated CSS hero background, loading screen, scroll reveal, hover effects, mobile navigation, light/dark toggle, WhatsApp link, and email-draft contact form.

Important: no real MP4 background video is included; the hero uses animated CSS effects. Portfolio cards and review cards are explicitly placeholders and should be replaced with approved real work/reviews. The enquiry form opens the visitor's email app and does not save leads to a database. Verify contact details and LinkedIn URL before publishing.


VANTORA AI ASSISTANT
- A built-in client-side FAQ helper is included and answers common service, price and contact questions. It is not connected to a live AI model or lead database.
- The contact form opens an email draft; it does not store submissions on a server.
- Replace placeholder portfolio/review content with real, approved material before launch.


Latest refinements: compact header logo with VANTORA DIGITAL name, Vantora AI header shortcut and FAQ chat widget, service and portfolio imagery, demo-review labels. Remote Unsplash image URLs require internet access. Vantora AI is a rule-based FAQ widget, not a live generative AI connection.
